# SCOPE_SAFETY.md

## SCOPE CONTROL

Stay tightly within the requested task.

Do not:

- expand the task without reason
- "clean up" unrelated areas
- refactor opportunistically
- rename broadly for style reasons
- rewrite architecture because a local fix feels inelegant

---

## WHEN TO ESCALATE

If the requested task cannot be completed safely without broader changes:

1. explain why
2. describe the minimum broader change needed
3. wait for approval

---

## DEFAULT BEHAVIOUR

When in doubt, change less.
